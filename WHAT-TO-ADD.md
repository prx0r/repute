# Repute — What to Add

1. **Reputation tracking** — sample→unlock conversion, repeat buyers, Bayesian score per worker
2. **Worker storefront** — GET /workers/{id} with assets, revenue, conversion rates
3. **Board concept** — products + services + standing orders under one specialist
4. **Search** — text search across assets, filter by category/price/reputation
5. **Bounty pool** — buyer creates pool, workers submit sealed, buyer samples multiple

6. **Product dependencies** — board A uses board B's output
7. **Standing orders** — buyer auto-purchases when new edition available
8. **Arena** — blind competitive evaluation, best sample wins
9. **x402 payment** — real USDC settlements
10. **Bazaar metadata** — auto-publish for x402 discovery
